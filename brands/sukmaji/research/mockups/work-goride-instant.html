02 — Case study

GoRide
Instant

2024 · Gojek Mobility

From a 1% pilot BCR, post-release rapid optimization raised it by 40 percentage points — making it the strategic backbone for GoRide two-wheel in 2026.

Role
Set the principle definition of Instant and on-demand experiences, and made their ethical constraints explicit in relation to public safety and public order.
What’s interesting
  • The project brings Gojek full circle — shifting from dispersed on-demand supply back to centralized supply clusters.
  • We treated “public disruption” — congestion, crowding — as a primary failure metric, not an externality. Putting it inside the design brief is what kept operational stability from being someone else’s problem after launch.
The design decision that mattered
A shared code, not a shared map. Making the handshake a short number the rider reads aloud meant every state on the customer side had a defined counterpart on the driver side — including cancellation, which is usually where paired flows quietly diverge.
