03 — Case study

Ads
Principles

2023 · Gojek Ads & Promo

A complete GoFood homepage redesign with embedded, highly contextualized ads shipped in five months (May–Sept 2023), taking ads to ~10% of overall GoFood revenue.

Role
Spearheaded the governance and operationalization of Gojek’s ads vertical, and codified its ethical principles with leadership buy-in.
What’s interesting
  • The principles were written to be binding: they enforce that we don’t abuse ads inventory for short-term gains, even in a quarter that would reward it. A principle that never costs anything isn’t a principle.
  • I built a framework for data-driven creative optimization that delegated effort across functions — which is what led to a dedicated Ads Operations team existing at all.
Governance as a design artefact
The output that outlived the launch is the audit process: seven functions, one accountable owner per step, and an explicit path from campaign proposal through QA to live. It was published at v0.3 with two lanes still marked with a question mark — because shipping the honest version invites the correction, and the polished version doesn’t.
